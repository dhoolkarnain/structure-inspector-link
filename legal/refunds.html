Cancelling

Open Plans & pricing in the app and choose Stop renewing. You will not be charged again, and you keep your plan until the end of the period you have paid for.

Refunds

  • If you were charged by mistake or twice, we refund the extra charge in full.
  • If you pay for a plan and cannot use it because of a fault on our side that we cannot fix, we refund the unused part of the period.
  • Otherwise, payments for a period already started are not refundable.

How refunds are paid
