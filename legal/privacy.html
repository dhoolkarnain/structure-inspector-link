What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, a password hash (we never store your password).

  • Field data you upload: photos, sensor and vibration records, scans, and AI detection results.

What we do not collect

Card, UPI and bank details go straight to Razorpay and never reach our servers.

Who sees it

Map data is requested from open services (OpenStreetMap, Overture Maps, satellite imagery and elevation providers) using the area you view. Photos you upload for defect detection may be processed by an AI service. Payments are processed by Razorpay under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data.
